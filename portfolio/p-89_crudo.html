Kan.Aria or Kan Aria was a fanzine created by Watodo, an art group formed in Udine, Italy, between 2002 and 2004, operating in Grado, Udine, and Milan (Italy). The zine was intended as a visual and physical manifesto, collecting photos, collages, poetry, and artistic intents. About the images below:
