"ABCBA". Video. Collaborative.
ABCBA is an account hosted by Vimeo.com video platform.
ABCBA is about audio-video actions.
ABCBA is a weekly drop collaborative art-object piece.
ABCBA is an invitation-based project.

We will share with you ABCBA login data.
You will be able to upload your original and
unpublished content.
The limit is 500 MB file size.
Your content will be online online 7 days.
After this period, during the day number eight, your content will be replaced with another video by another author.
ABCBA works around two features intended for a basic account / basic membership:
0) Upload 500 Mb of video matter per week / 25 Gb per year
1) Replacing a file with a different version of the movie, maintaining the same hyperlink.
Each video will be a version of a single-whole piece.
This is a reasoning towards a tool and a possibility of a device, and in a wide sense, towards the contemporary and cultural scenario of which we are inhabitants.
And, not the least, about friendship. This project rises with the collaboration of dear friends. We’re grateful to all of them.
ABCBA is a documenting crossover video action project.
Each author will remain the only owner of his content.
Each movie can be recorded using the best and comfortable tools for you.
The main aspect of ABCBA is sharing an action (your action and experience).
There is no required editing, color correction, postproduction.
Just press 'Rec' and 'Stop'. Do it your way.
